Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2025

1. Introduction

At Livenest, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website.

2. Data Collection

We collect the following types of information when you use Livenest:

  • Personal information (e.g., name, email, date of birth).
  • Usage data (e.g., browsing patterns, device information).
  • Interaction data (e.g., feed posts, comments, reactions).

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve our services.
  • To communicate with you regarding your account or updates.
  • To personalize your experience and enhance platform interactions.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Use of Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. These cookies may track your preferences and help us improve the site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

We may share your data with trusted third-party services like payment gateways or email services, but they will only use it for the purpose of providing the services you requested.

6. Data Sharing

Your personal data will not be shared with any third party except as outlined in this Privacy Policy or when required by law.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

8. User Rights

  • You have the right to access, update, and delete your personal data.
  • You can request to withdraw consent at any time.
  • If you are in the EU, you have additional rights under the GDPR.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide you with our services or until you request deletion, whichever is sooner.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred and stored outside your country of residence. By using Livenest, you consent to this transfer.

11. Children’s Privacy

Livenest does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by posting the updated policy on our website or via email.

13. Opt-Out

You have the option to opt-out of promotional emails and other forms of marketing communications by clicking on the unsubscribe link provided in those emails.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about your privacy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@livenest.com.

15. Legal Compliance

Livenest complies with all applicable data protection laws, including the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)**, and **Indian Personal Data Protection Bill 2019**.
