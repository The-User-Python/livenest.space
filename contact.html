Get in Touch

For all communications, please email us at: info@livenest.com

Our team will respond to your email as soon as possible. Please ensure your message includes:

  • Your full name
  • A clear subject line
  • Detailed information regarding your inquiry or feedback

Communication Guidelines

To help us assist you effectively, please adhere to the following guidelines when contacting us:

  • Use a valid email address that we can reply to.
  • Provide as much detail as possible about your query or concern.
  • Refrain from using offensive or inappropriate language.
  • Avoid sending multiple emails for the same issue; we will get back to you as soon as we can.

Failure to follow these guidelines may result in delays or non-response to your inquiry.

What Not to Do

To maintain a respectful and efficient communication channel, please avoid:

  • Sending unsolicited promotional content or spam.
  • Using inappropriate or offensive language.
  • Sharing personal or sensitive information that is not necessary for your inquiry.
  • Contacting multiple team members separately about the same issue.
