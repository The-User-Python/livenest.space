User Rights & Responsibilities

Know your rights and limitations while using Livenest.

1. Your Rights as a Livenest User

  • 1.1 Account Ownership: You have full control over your account and can update, edit, or delete your profile at any time.
  • 1.2 Content Posting: You can post feeds, share updates, and interact with other users within the community guidelines.
  • 1.3 Privacy Control: You can manage who sees your posts, profile details, and personal information through privacy settings.
  • 1.4 Data Access: You have the right to download your data or request account deletion as per our Privacy Policy.
  • 1.5 Report & Block: You have the right to report inappropriate behavior and block users who violate community standards.

2. User Responsibilities

  • 2.1 Real Name Policy: Users must register with their real name. Fake or misleading identities are not allowed.
  • 2.2 No Harmful Content: Users must not post hate speech, abusive content, or anything that violates community guidelines.
  • 2.3 No Spam or Misuse: Posting repetitive, misleading, or irrelevant content that disrupts the platform is prohibited.
  • 2.4 No Unauthorized Promotions: Users cannot promote third-party businesses or services without permission.
  • 2.5 Security Compliance: Users should not attempt to hack, exploit, or misuse Livenest’s systems or other users’ data.

3. Future Features

We are continuously improving Livenest and plan to introduce exciting new features, including:

  • Add Friends: Connect with people and expand your network.
  • Direct Chat: Send private messages and have real-time conversations.
  • Profile Customization: Personalize your profile with themes.
  • Live Streaming: Share live moments with the community.
  • Marketplace: Buy, sell, and trade items within the community.
  • Blue Tick: Buy, and get verify checkmark on your profile for 1 month.
